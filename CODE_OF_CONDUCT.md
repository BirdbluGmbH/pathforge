# Code of Conduct

This repository is a support and issue tracker for PathForge (Base) and
PathForge Pro. It is not a general discussion forum.

## Ground rules

- Be respectful. Discuss the issue, not the person.
- Keep reports factual and reproducible: use the provided issue templates,
  include versions and the `DnskNative.Diagnose()` output.
- No marketing, spam, or link drops.
- No personal data of third parties (customers, players) in reports —
  redact before posting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or close issues that violate these rules, and may
block repeat offenders. Decisions are final and will not be discussed in the
issue thread; contact [info@birdblu.com](mailto:info@birdblu.com) if you
believe an action was taken in error.
